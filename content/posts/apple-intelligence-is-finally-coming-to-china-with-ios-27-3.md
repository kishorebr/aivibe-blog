<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Apple will launch Apple Intelligence in China with iOS 27.3, the company announced <a href="https://www.apple.com.cn/newsroom/2026/10/apple-intelligence-features-are-coming-to-users-in-china/">on its Chinese newsroom website</a>, as <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/30/apple-says-ios-27-3-beta-coming-next-week/">reported by <em>MacRumors</em></a>. The Apple Intelligence features will be available first in an iOS 27.3 beta that's set to launch on November 4th, with a full iOS 27.3 release scheduled for December.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This launch doesn't bring the new Siri AI to China. Instead, Apple is launching several of its earlier Apple Intelligence features like notification summaries, real-time translation, rewriting and proofreading tools, visual intelligence, and removing objects from photos.
