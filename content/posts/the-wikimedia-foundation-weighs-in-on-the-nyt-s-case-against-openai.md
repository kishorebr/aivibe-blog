                The Wikimedia Foundation has weighed in on the copyright lawsuit launched by The New York Times against OpenAI and Microsoft. The people behind Wikipedia have filed an amicus brief urging nuance in the case, which seeks to decide whether or not OpenAI and Microsoft's use of content published in The New York Times for any purpose, including AI model training, is a violation of copyright law.
The New York Times says that companies like OpenAI shouldn't be given free reign over any of their published content. OpenAI and Microsoft say that training and deploying AI models based on published data is necessary for technological development and would therefore fall within the definition of fair use.
