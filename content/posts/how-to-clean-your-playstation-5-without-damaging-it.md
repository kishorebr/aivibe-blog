                Cleaning a PlayStation 5 isn't as exciting as playing its best games, but it's important to do on occasion. With PlayStation 5 prices climbing, you don't want to suddenly be in the market for a new console because of a total lack of maintenance. Dust buildup in the system restricts airflow and makes your system work harder to cool itself. But overzealous cleaning can be dangerous, too. 
When cleaning your PS5, it's best to keep it simple and only go deeper if needed. Never start right after using the system. Prepare it for its dust exorcism by shutting it down, unplugging all cables and letting it cool before setting it on a flat, clean surface. To start, wipe off the outside surfaces to remove any dust or grit visible from the exterior. Don't use bleach or chemically treated cloths, and you shouldn't be excessively forceful while cleaning.
