<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For the 14th time, a Google product has hit 1 billion users. Google CEO Sundar Pichai <a href="https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2087222656819241292">posted on X</a> that a billion people are using Gemini every month, and that Gemini is Google's fastest-growing product ever.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A billion users is a huge milestone, but Google isn't the first AI app to hit it. OpenAI's ChatGPT hit the mark a few weeks ago, though the company buried the announcement that "more than 1 billion people are putting ChatGPT to work" in an <a href="https://openai.com/index/how-the-world-is-putting-chatgpt-to-work/">otherwise anodyne blog post</a> about how people use AI. <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/chatgpt-app-hits-1-billion-monthly-active-users-record-time-data-shows-2026-06-02/">External data suggested</a> ChatGPT crossed 1 billion users as early as this June, but OpenAI hadn't announced anything until that post on August 6th.  …</p>
