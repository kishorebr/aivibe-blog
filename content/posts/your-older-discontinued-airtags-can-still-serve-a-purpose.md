                Earlier this year, Apple introduced the first new generation AirTag since the original debuted in 2021. And while you might still be able to find first-generation Apple AirTags for purchase on some websites, Apple doesn't sell them anymore. The company is now focusing solely on the new model and leaving the original AirTag behind. But the old ones are still supported in the Find My app, so there's lots of ways you can continue to use them.
The newer Apple AirTag offers an expanded finding range thanks to the second-generation ultrawideband chip, upgraded Bluetooth chip, improved Precision Finding from up to 50 percent farther away and a louder speaker. Given how cheap Apple AirTags are ($29 on Amazon) and all the upgrades with the second-gen model, you'll probably want to grab a few for your most precious items.
