Nvidia thinks a new partnership built on open-source software is the answer. On Monday, the company announced the Open Secure AI Alliance, which it said "will work to remediate and disclose vulnerabilities using open technologies." The announcement is something of a response to Project Glasswing, the security alliance Anthropic spearheaded around its highly capable Mythos 5 model.
