                If your Fitbit is acting up, the first thing you might do is restart it. But is that a good idea? Rest assured, regularly restarting your Fitbit shouldn't do any harm to it. It's similar to restarting your laptop when it starts to lag or you run out of memory resources, or restarting your router because the connection is spotty or not working at all. There's a reason, after all, that two common IT fixes are to turn a device off and back on again or run a restart.
Restarting your Fitbit can often times fix whatever issue might be going on with the device. Maybe it's failing to sync to the app, or the screen is not responding, completely frozen. Maybe your swipes or taps aren't registering, there's a delay or it doesn't seem to be tracking stats properly.
