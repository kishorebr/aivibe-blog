<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An unsettling Alexa Plus bug has seen some Amazon Echo smart home speakers reduced to saying - and sometimes singing - nothing but "lalala" on repeat for minutes at a time, often in the middle of conversations with users. When quizzed about the behavior, Alexa is apparently entirely unaware of what it's been doing.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The issue has been reported on Reddit repeatedly over the last few weeks, <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/alexa/comments/1www1qs/alexa_did_something_very_strange_today/">as recently as three days ago</a>, across multiple types of Echo speaker. Multiple users have <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/alexa/comments/1wk4rii/alexa_said_lalalalala_for_10_minutes/">uploaded videos</a> of the speakers' strange behavior.
