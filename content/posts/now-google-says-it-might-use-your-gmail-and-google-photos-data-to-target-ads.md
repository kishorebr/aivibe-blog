<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Google has updated <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en-US#infocollect">its privacy policy</a> in a way that could mean you'll start seeing targeted ads based on your personal data if you're using personalized AI features. The update, spotted by <a href="https://www.androidauthority.com/google-personal-intelligence-privacy-3727111/"><em>Android Authority</em></a>, adds an exception to its promise about not using your Drive, Gmail, or Google Photos for personalized ads. While the previous version simply said it wouldn't do that, as of October 16th, Google has added the line, "unless you choose to make data from these services available to other Google services that support personalized ads."</p>
