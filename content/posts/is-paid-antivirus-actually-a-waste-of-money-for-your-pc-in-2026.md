                Windows was not the safest operating system in the early days. Back then, malware and viruses were a risk that you were required to avoid and resolve on your own. As attacks grew, a whole antivirus industry spun up to provide protection for millions of PC users. Suites like McAfee, AVG and Norton were common options people installed to keep their computers safe.
Times have moved on, though. We've had built-in antivirus protection on PCs since Windows 8 launched in 2012. But third-party antivirus software still exists, along with apps offering more complex anti-malware protection. While there can be uses for these other tools, protection is now baked into your PC from the first day you boot, so you don't need to pay for it.
