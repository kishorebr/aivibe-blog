<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After launching its <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004096/openai-chatgpt-dots-hands-on-agent">Dots AI agents platform</a> earlier this year, OpenAI is <a href="https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/">launching a wallet</a> for the agents. ChatGPT Wallet is <a href="https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet">rolling out starting today</a> for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US aged 18 and older using a US billing address and "eligible US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa cards."</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After adding a card to the wallet, your Dots agents can use it to buy things in response to your prompts, but they'll check in with you each time before checking out. OpenAI's <a href="https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet">support page</a> says every purchase with ChatGPT Wallet requires a security check with biometric verification like Face ID or a fingerprint scan.</p>
