                Your 3D printer can spend hours making a part, only for it to detach from the bed halfway through. Worse, the finished piece might look great but refuse to fit where it should. That's a lot of filament down the drain, all because the printer might need calibration.
An uneven bed, incorrect nozzle height and poorly tuned extrusion can cause adhesion issues, rough surfaces and dimensional inaccuracies in an FDM 3D printer. Calibration can address those problems on both older printers that need manual adjustments and newer models that handle some checks automatically. It's also worth checking calibration after moving your printer, adjusting belts, replacing a nozzle or performing mechanical maintenance.
