<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Microsoft just wrapped up a big Windows and Surface-focused keynote in San Francisco.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The biggest announcement was arguably the release details about <a href="https://www.theverge.com/news/1006378/microsoft-surface-laptop-ultra-pricing-release-date">the Surface Laptop Ultra</a>, its new laptop that&#8217;s powered by Nvidia&#8217;s RTX Spark Arm-based chip. The machine will start at $2,599 for a configuration with an 8-core CPU, 24GB of RAM, and 512GB of storage, and it will be released on October 16th. The laptop also has <a href="https://www.theverge.com/news/1006445/microsofts-surface-laptop-ultra-has-built-in-magnetic-usb-c-charging">built-in magnetic USB-C charging</a> instead of the proprietary Surface Connect magnetic charging port. <em>The Verge</em>&#8216;s Tom Warren published <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/1006356/microsoft-surface-laptop-ultra-behind-the-scenes">a deep dive all about the new laptop</a>. Microsoft <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/1006915/microsoft-surface-rtx-spark-dev-box-preorder">also announced</a> that the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box, a mini PC for developers, is available for preorder for $5,999 ahead of shipping in November.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In addition, Microsoft shared details about <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/1007113/microsoft-windows-copilot-ai-control-search-hybrid-intelligence">some updates coming to Windows</a>, including &#8220;Hybrid Intelligence&#8221; features that lets Copilot use files on your Windows PC to take actions and a new search experience that lets you do quick actions from the search bar.</p>
