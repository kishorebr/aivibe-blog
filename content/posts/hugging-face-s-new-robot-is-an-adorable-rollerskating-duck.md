<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Hugging Face's Pollen Robotics has launched its second cute AI robot, <a href="https://pollen-robotics.com/microduck/blog/introducing-microduck/">the Microduck</a>, a one-eyed biped standing just under 10 inches tall. It's available to preorder now for $399 in cream, graphite, lavender, and sky blue, and Pollen Robotics says it plans to start shipping the little robot "before Christmas 2026." </p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://pollen-robotics.com/microduck/">Video demos</a> of the Microduck show it picking up socks and markers, kicking around a ball, and zipping around on tiny rollerskates. Pollen Robotics says it can also "react to its surroundings" and follow around a laser pointer, or users can control it with a game controller.
