                Sean Cairncross is no longer the US National Cyber Director. The Trump administration has fired him, following several tech outages in the country, according to Reuters. If you'll recall, Microsoft's services recently went down and remained offline for almost a full day, which had affected businesses, hospitals and even governments around the world. The Federal Aviation Administration had also ordered a nationwide ground stop that had disrupted more than 18,000 flights inside, to and from the US. 
It's not quite clear why the White House removed Cairncross in relation to those incidents, but the government has been getting flak over its response to cybersecurity threats, including those posed by advanced AI agents and tools.
