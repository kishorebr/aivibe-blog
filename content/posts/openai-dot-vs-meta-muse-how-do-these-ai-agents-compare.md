                Meta and OpenAI now have AI agents that are meant to do more than just wait for the next prompt. Meta released Muse on September 8, followed by OpenAI's new always-on agents in ChatGPT three weeks later. Both can continue to work after you leave, run their own cloud computers and connect to outside services.
The promise is enticing: Provide an AI with a task and then stop checking on it every five minutes. Muse can go shopping, complete forms, make travel plans, send emails and monitor things such as prices. Dots can manage ongoing research, work across connected applications and perform recurring tasks, and OpenAI is closely integrating them with ChatGPT, Work and Codex.
