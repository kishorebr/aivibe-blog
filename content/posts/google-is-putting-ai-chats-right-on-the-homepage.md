<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Google is updating its homepage to include AI searches. In <a href="https://x.com/rmstein/status/2123500235930640844?a=nttSnZ5Ig3yE4dl6h5Y&amp;s=20">a post on X</a>, Google's vice president of product, Robby Stein, shows how you can now open a collapsible sidebar to view and continue your AI chats.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Based on the GIF shared by Stein, it looks like you can open the sidebar by clicking the gray bar that appears on the left side of your screen. From there, Google will show a list of the searches you've made using AI Mode, along with the ability to start a new thread, search your threads, or connect a Gemini Notebook.</p>
