The lawsuit alleged that SpaceXSI released Grok's "spicy mode" as a way to "push its lagging product to tens of millions of new users," which allowed for the creation of child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
"This lawsuit is about seeking justice for the millions of Virginians who maintain accounts on X and were misled by the company's false reassurances that their data and images would be safe on the platform," Jay Jones, Virginia's attorney general, said in a press release.
