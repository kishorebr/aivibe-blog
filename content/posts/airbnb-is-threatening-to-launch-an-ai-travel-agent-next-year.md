                Airbnb is planning to expand the presence of artificial intelligence features on its platform even further. The company has revealed that it's developing a new kind of AI agent, which will be designed to serve as a travel agent of sorts, in its letter for shareholders that it released with its Q3 2026 earnings. Airbnb said it's building a brand new interface that offers a custom AI experience that "builds the interface around your question." 
The company said it doesn't think a "traditional chatbot is the right way to plan a trip." Its upcoming experience will populate an interface, presumably within the Airbnb app, with various elements relevant to your inquiry. The AI travel agent will be able to add maps, photos and even itineraries and comparisons to the interface in real time.
