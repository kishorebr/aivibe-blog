                Gmail is no longer just an email service. Plenty of websites and apps let you log in with your Google account, and many people use that one login for almost everything they do online. It makes signing in a lot easier. It also means that whoever holds the keys to your account holds a lot more than just your private emails.
Google is very aware of that, which is why it offers a variety of security and privacy settings to keep your account safe and keep third parties out of it. These are some of the most important ones you should be using.
