                Have you ever heard your AirPods chirp randomly even though you weren't doing anything? The most common reason why your AirPods are trying to get your attention is that they're low in battery. You can easily check if that's what's going on by flipping their case open and looking at the phone they're paired with. However, your AirPods could be emitting sounds due to other reasons, and those sounds could range from cute little chirps to ear-piercing screeches.
