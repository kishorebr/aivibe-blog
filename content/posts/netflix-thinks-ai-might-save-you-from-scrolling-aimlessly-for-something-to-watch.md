                Netflix is adding an AI-powered recommendation tool to its TV app as it tries to get viewers to stop scrolling so much and watch more shows and movies on the platform. The company framed AI Discover as a testbed for new features that it might make part of the core Netflix experience. If it's available on your account, you'll see a Discover tab with a red gem icon on the top navigation bar.
The first thing Netflix is trying out in AI Discover is theme-based recommendations. It will display an array of themes along with a relevant clip from a show or movie. The themes are based on things like who you might be watching with (in which case a family movie night option might work best), what you're in the mood for (e.g. a horror flick or light-hearted comedy) or titles that won't take up your entire evening.
