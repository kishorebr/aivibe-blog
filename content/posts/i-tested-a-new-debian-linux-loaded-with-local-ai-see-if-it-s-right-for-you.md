There are, however, those who believe it is an inevitability and have fully embraced the concept. Even Linux creator Linus Torvalds has told anti-AI developers to "fork off."Now, one developer has taken Debian and twisted it into a local AI workstation that would make a great tool for your AI needs. Use it for general AI chats, to help you develop apps, or just about anything where AI could assist.Also: Linus Torvalds puts his foot down, tells anti-AI programmers to 'fork it'The distribution is Shadowfetch. Before I continue, however, a caveat: This distro is in the very early stages of development, and it appears to have only one developer working on it, which means it might progress very slowly. Even so, it's already pretty impressive (even in these early stages). Therefore, I don't yet recommend Shadowfetch   for anything more than satisfying your curiosity. I had tons of trouble getting Shadowfetch up and working (the installation failed every time, so I had to run it from a live instance).
