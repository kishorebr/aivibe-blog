<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As advanced and <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2017/5/29/15702592/pandora-world-of-avatar-navi-river-journey-ride-review">lifelike as the animatronic characters</a> at Disney parks have become, they typically either stick to predefined movements and routines, or there's an <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/895369/olaf-disney-imagineering-frozen-robot-up-close">out of sight puppeteer</a> helping make their behavior appear more spontaneous. That could soon change as <a href="https://www.theverge.com/streaming/827402/disney-rd">Walt Disney Imagineering R&amp;D</a> has revealed <a href="https://disneyexperiences.com/anzellans-imagineering-rd-robotic-characters/">three new Audio-Animatronic prototypes</a> that rely on Amazon's AWS cloud and AI capabilities to respond in unique ways to guest interactions in real-time.</p>
