                It's been over 20 years since the Disney-owned ABC has aired a Super Bowl game, but that changes on February 14. ABC will run the broadcast live, along with ESPN. However, it has just been announced that it'll also stream on Disney+.
This should make it easy for just about anyone to tune in, as the livestream will be available in 55 global markets. The platform will air the event in both English and Spanish.

In addition to the Super Bowl, Disney+ will be streaming two Monday Night Football games throughout the season. This begins on October 26 when the Philadelphia Eagles host the Dallas Cowboys. The other match has yet to be announced.
