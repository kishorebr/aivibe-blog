<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Google has released its latest frontier model, Gemini 4 Carbon, offering "significant gains" in coding, agentic tasks, and cybersecurity. In <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/">an announcement on Wednesday</a>, Google highlights Carbon's top spot on the <a href="https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index">Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v5.0)</a> benchmark, with Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra trailing behind.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The new Gemini 4 Carbon model offers improvements over Argon, which Google released earlier this month. It's currently only available to a select group of cybersecurity defense companies, but it will eventually expand to other partners <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/safety-security/fairwind-program/">in its Fairwind Program</a>.</p>
