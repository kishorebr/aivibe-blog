                A liquid cooling system can be one of the star components of a custom-built gaming PC, but are they really worth the money? After all, they're usually a lot more expensive than traditional air coolers. AIO (all-in-one) liquid cooling systems often look flashy and can sometimes be tricky to install, but there's a reason they're all the rage with high-end PC builders. 
Let's get into how liquid coolers work, the main differences between them and traditional heatsink models, and when it makes sense to opt for an AIO system over a tried-and-true air cooler.
