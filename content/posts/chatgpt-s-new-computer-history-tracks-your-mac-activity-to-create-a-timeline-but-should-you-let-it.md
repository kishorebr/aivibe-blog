    ChatGPT's new Computer History tracks your Mac activity to create a timeline -  but should you let it?
     
    Rolling out to ChatGPT's Mac app, Computer History creates a timeline from your activities across the apps and websites you use on your Mac. Is that a privacy risk?
      Written by 
            Lance Whitney, ContributorContributor  Aug. 14, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. PT                            Lance Whitney/ZDNETFollow ZDNET: Add us as a preferred source on Google.ZDNET's key takeawaysChatGPT's Computer History creates a timeline from your app and website activities.From the timeline, you can incorporate your work into your conversations.The option is only in the Mac app and only for certain types of accounts.ChatGPT now has a new feature that sounds like a productivity boost but may trigger concerns among privacy-minded users.On Thursday, OpenAI introduced its new Computer History feature. Now hitting the ChatGPT Mac app, but only for Pro, Business, and Enterprise accounts, Computer History is an opt-in skill that can capture your activities across the apps and websites you use on your Mac. Those activities are then turned into memories and a timeline where you can incorporate them into your conversations and research with ChatGPT and the Codex coding tool.Also: How to use ChatGPT: A beginner's guide to mastering OpenAI's chatbotThe feature is rolling out globally in most regions, but with access in the UK, Switzerland, and the European Economic Area to follow in the next few weeks.(Disclosure: Ziff Davis, ZDNET's parent company, filed an April 2025 lawsuit against OpenAI, alleging it infringed Ziff Davis copyrights in training and operating its AI systems.)Replaces Chronicle, no screenshots requiredComputer History builds on and replaces the ChatGPT Chronicle feature, which can monitor your screen activity to understand what you're doing while you work.
