                Google is allowing its Gemini-powered dictation tech to trickle down to the Pixel 10 series, meaning those who haven't yet upgraded to the Pixel 11 series get one of its standout features.
Rambler is a text input feature that takes your natural language ramblings and turns them into coherent, grammatically correct text. It also strips out the "umms" and "ahhs" from your dictation, as well as unnecessary words, and if you self-correct while speaking, Gemini will smoothen it all out in the final passage.
Despite by his own admission not being much of a voice texter, Engadget's Nathan Ingraham praised the keyboard-native feature in his Pixel 11 review, so it's good to see it coming to more devices.
