                There's something oddly thrilling (and a little terrifying) about steering a 6,000-pound trailer around a parking lot with nothing more than touchscreen controls on an iPad. It's also the perfect encapsulation of what makes the Pebble Flow electric trailer unlike any other RV out there.
With integrated solar panels, electric motors and automated hitching, the tech-forward trailer is meant to simplify many of the complexities and annoyances that often come with RV ownership. That's Pebble's promise, anyway.
I've been intrigued by the Pebble Flow since my colleagues first saw it on the CES showfloor in 2024. The futuristic-looking RV has a sleek, minimalist interior, a dedicated workspace and full bathroom.
