                Amazon's new Alexa Tablets, square-one reimaginings of the company's affordable tablet lineup, owe a lot to the iPad. And how could they not? Apple's family of tablets has become synonymous with the form factor globally, so why wouldn't Amazon take inspiration from their slim, all-aluminum designs and high-quality screens? Nowhere is that inspiration more apparent, though, than in the Alexa Tablet 12 Pro, the most premium of Amazon's new options and a clear competitor to the iPad Pro.
The Alexa Tablet 12 Pro features a 12-inch, high-refresh rate LCD screen, support for keyboard and stylus accessories that make it a potential productivity tool and the Play Store, a proper place to get premium apps after Fire Tablets were saddled with the limited selection in the Amazon Appstore for years.
