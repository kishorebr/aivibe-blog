                Rockstar Games workers in the UK have overwhelmingly voted against a union recognition effort, with three-quarters rejecting an agreement with the IWGB Rockstar Game Workers Union. The vote took place at the same time the company released Grand Theft Auto 6, which smashed records as it generated $3.79 billion in pre-orders and first-day sales.
Over 93 percent of Rockstar workers across offices in Edinburgh, Leeds, Lincoln, Dundee and London took part in the two-week ballot. Of those, 1,163 turned down formal recognition of their branch of the IWGB (Independent Workers Union of Great Britain), while 381 were in favor, according to IGN.
The Rockstar Game Workers Union will continue to organize without a formal agreement with the company. However, the result means Rockstar management won't be obligated to officially recognize the union or hold regular meetings with representatives.
