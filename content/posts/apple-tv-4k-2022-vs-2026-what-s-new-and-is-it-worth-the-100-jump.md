                It's taken Apple four years to bring us a new version of the Apple TV 4K, and along with it comes a now all-too-familiar price rise. The fourth-gen box Apple announced today is $299, a $100 jump over the cheapest previous-gen model. The sticker price is actually more than twice what the 2022 version cost at the start of the year, before the summer price increase. It's set to be released on November 13 with a faster A19 chip and built-in mics for talking to Siri without the remote.
If you've already got the 2022 or short-lived 2021 model, there's some good news: Siri AI, the chattier assistant Apple has built this launch around, is coming to your box in an update. And the new remote, the one that's much harder to lose, supports older devices and is being sold on its own.
