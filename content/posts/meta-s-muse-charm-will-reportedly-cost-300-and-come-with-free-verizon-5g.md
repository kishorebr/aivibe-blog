                Meta reportedly plans to sell the Muse Charm, a Tamagotchi-esque gadget for talking to its Muse AI agent, for $300, according to The Information. The company introduced the Muse Charm at Meta Connect in September as a way to use its agent without having to reach for a smartphone, and said it would launch in time for the holidays.
At Connect, CEO Mark Zuckerberg said the Muse Charm packs "the whole Muse experience, including the real-time voice and avatar stack into something that fits on a keychain and is always available to talk to." The device features a fingerprint reader to verify your identity before you're able to give commands and will come with up to 12 months of free 5G data from Verizon, The Information reports.
Engadget has asked Meta to comment on The Information's report. We'll update this article if we hear back.
