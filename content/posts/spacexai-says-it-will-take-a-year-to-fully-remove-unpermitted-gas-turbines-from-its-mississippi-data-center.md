                SpaceX will fully remove the unpermitted gas turbines it's using to power SpaceXAI data center operations near Memphis, Tennessee by July 2027, according to a new company update spotted by TechCrunch. The turbines are currently the subject of a NAACP lawsuit that alleges the company is exposing nearby communities to hazardous chemicals and pollution.
The company says a new 1.2GW power plant it's constructing under a Clean Air Act permit in Mississippi will ultimately replace the 69 unpermitted gas turbines it's currently relying on. As part of an agreed order with the Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality, removal of the temporary turbines will begin in August 2026, though SpaceX says the process won't be fully complete until July 2027.
SpaceX acquired xAI in February 2026, with big plans to launch data centers into space, alongside its pre-existing commitment to rockets and satellite internet.
