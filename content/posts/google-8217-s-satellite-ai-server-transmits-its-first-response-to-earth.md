<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Google's AI chip-equipped satellite has generated its first response from space. In a blog post on Friday, <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/project-suncatcher-satellite-response/">Google says</a> it successfully booted up the satellite's Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), allowing the on-board Gemma 4 model to follow through on a prompt to create a haiku about being in space:</p>
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Blue marble below</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Data streams in silent grace</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Starlight feeds my core. &#10024;</p>
</blockquote>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Google says it tried out other space-themed limericks and telemetry prompts as well. The test is part of Google's Project Suncatcher, <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/1000015/google-ai-satellite-space-project-suncatcher">an experimental initiative</a> that could eventually put AI data centers in space.
