                Valve has announced that it's completed all of the waitlist orders for the smaller 512GB model of the Steam Machine and is now letting anyone buy it while supplies last. The change should make the experience of buying the Linux gaming PC a bit closer to the consoles it competes with, though Valve's announcement doesn't suggest dealing with RAMaggedon has gotten any easier.
It's not clear how many 512GB Steam Machines are available, but rather than set up another waitlist, the company says it will mark the model as "out of stock" once it sells through its current supply. Things are a bit more complicated for the 2TB model. Valve has "finished getting purchase invites out to all reservation holders" and anyone who joined the waitlist later will be able to keep their place in line.
