<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Apple will now let Siri AI perform actions across third-party apps. As part of the iOS 27.3 update rolling out on Tuesday, Apple <a href="https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=gdk2umrf">announced developers</a> can now integrate Siri AI's new features in "all apps," allowing the voice assistant to draw on a user's personal context, process natural language prompts, and use on-screen awareness to complete requests.</p>
