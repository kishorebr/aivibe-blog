<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On Thursday, Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro signed the <a href="https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/text/PDF/2025/0/SB0806/PN1808">Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act</a> into law, requiring disclosures on AI-generated ads. The law requires any ad - whether it's a still image, video, audio, or text - that "has been significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence" to include a label in the same format as the ad itself. That applies to <a href="https://www.theverge.com/news/812559/coca-cola-ai-holiday-christmas-commercial-2025">TV commercials from multi-billion-dollar companies</a>, as well as restaurants' <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/989376/ai-generated-food-why-does-it-look-like-that">ugly, crowded flyers</a> about their wing specials.</p>
