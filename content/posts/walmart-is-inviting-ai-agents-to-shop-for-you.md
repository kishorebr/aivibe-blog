<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Walmart will soon let AI agents shop on its website for you. Next month, <a href="https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/10/Making-It-Possible-for-Agents-to-Shop-on-Behalf-of-Customers">the retail giant will allow you</a> to use an AI agent on the site, with systems in place to let you approve and review purchases and set spending limits.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The embrace of AI agents stands in stark contrast to Amazon, <a href="https://www.theverge.com/news/813755/amazon-perplexity-ai-shopping-agent-block">which blocked Perplexity's shopping agent</a> and <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/998078/amazon-blocks-meta-muse-ai-agent-shopping">Meta's Muse AI assistant</a>. But Walmart is opening its doors to AI shoppers - as long as they follow the retailer's rules. It has outlined a set of terms for agents, saying that "agents must not take actions beyond what the shopper intends" and cannot change a customer's payment method.
