<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang apologized to staff during his first all-hands since returning to the company, while attempting to distance himself from the narrative that the White House and tech leaders helped him get his job back, according to <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-24/nvidia-s-huang-to-host-all-hands-meeting-a-day-after-his-return">reports from <em>Bloomberg</em></a><em>,</em> <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/24/technology/jensen-huang-apology-nvidia-ceo.html"><em>The New York Times</em></a>, and <a href="https://bsky.app/profile/marisakabas.bsky.social/post/3n33bk6pymk2s"><em>The Handbasket's</em> Marisa Kabas</a>.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">During the meeting, Huang reportedly called claims that President Donald Trump was directly involved in his reinstatement - an idea <a href="https://www.theverge.com/policy/1040096/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang-reinstated">publicly promoted by Trump himself</a> - "completely untrue." He added, "Don't think for a second politics played a role in the company. We are the most apolitical company," <a href="https://bsky.app/profile/marisakabas.bsky.social/post/3n33bk6pymk2s">according to Kabas</a>.</p>
