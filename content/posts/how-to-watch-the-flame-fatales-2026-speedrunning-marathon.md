                Summer is winding down, but gamers haven't turned down the heat. This Sunday is the start of Flame Fatales, a week-long charity speedrunning marathon raising money for Malala Fund. The event is run by Frame Fatales, a community under the Games Done Quick banner that spotlights and uplifts women and femmes in speedrunning.
Flame Fatales starts on September 13 at 12:30PM ET and lasts until September 19. The show will stream live on the GDQ channels on both Twitch and YouTube during the week. The Flame Fatales marathon isn't a 24/7 affair like the mainstage GDQ events; instead, each day's gaming begins at 1PM ET and usually wraps by midnight, so be sure to check each day's schedule if there's anything in particular you're keen to watch.
