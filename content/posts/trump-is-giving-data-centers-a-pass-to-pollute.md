<p class="wp-block-paragraph">President Donald Trump is weakening environmental regulations in the name of speeding up the construction of AI data centers, raising health risks for Americans, a cadre of former EPA officials said this week in a briefing and new <a href="https://www.environmentalprotectionnetwork.org/new-report-ai-data-center-boom-poses-growing-health-risks-as-epa-other-federal-agencies-weaken-protections-against-harmful-pollution/">report</a>. They are urging - perhaps futilely - the president to adopt a "Data Center Health Protection Pledge" to signal that the administration is taking the environmental threat seriously. </p>
