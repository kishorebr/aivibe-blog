                Thanksgiving is supposed to be a time for rest, relaxation and appreciating all the good things that have happened to you over the past year. However, there's no denying that it's also a ton of work for whoever is in charge of preparing your Thanksgiving meal. Not only do you have to consider the cooking itself, there's all the other things that come with making a holiday feast like choosing the right recipes, gathering all the ingredients and scheduling which dishes need to be cooked at what time so everything is ready together. And all that doesn't even include managing anyone who feels like helping, which can turn into its own logistical issue.
So to help smooth out the chaos that is holiday cooking, we've put together a collection of essential cooking gadgets that can save you time and take some of the stress out of preparing your Thanksgiving bounty.
