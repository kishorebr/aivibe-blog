                Google has revealed changes to its Gemini apps and website on its support page, which could impact how you use the AI chatbot if you're not paying for a subscription. Starting on October 9, you will no longer be able to access the Gemini Flash and Pro models if you're on a free account. At the moment, you'll see three options in the drop-down menu at the top of the app or right next to the text box on desktop. But in a few days, all your free inquiries on Gemini will use Flash-Lite, Google's most cost-effective model. Flash is a step up from Lite, while Pro was designed for advanced reasoning and more complex tasks. 
If you're paying for Google AI Plus, the company's cheapest subscription tier that costs $5 a month in the US, you will be losing access to the Gemini 3.1 Pro model, as well.
