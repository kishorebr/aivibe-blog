                We've been hearing a lot about Apple's renewed smart home endeavors from Mark Gurman at Bloomberg over the past few months, and he's back today with a report that the company co-developed several new gadgets in partnership with LG Electronics. Based on recent regulatory filings by LG, Bloomberg reports that the results of this collaboration are expected to include a smart doorbell, a smart thermostat, a smart deadbolt lock, indoor and outdoor security cameras, and a floodlight camera. Rather than Apple's iconic fruit symbol, the hardware will reportedly be sold under LG's banner, with the South Korean business responsible for the devices' manufacturing and product support.
