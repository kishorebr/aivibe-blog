<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Suno is facing yet another <a href="https://variety.com/2026/music/news/elvis-eminem-sue-suno-ai-music-generator-1236941798/">pair of lawsuits</a>. A group of rights holders of Elvis Presley's music and the publisher of some of Eminem's biggest records are suing the AI music company, claiming it illegally generates music and lyrics that mimic those artists. The lawyers in the Presley suit claim that Suno has violated Tennessee's Ensuring Likeness Voice and Image Security (ELVIS) Act, which makes it illegal for an AI company to clone a singer's voice.</p>
