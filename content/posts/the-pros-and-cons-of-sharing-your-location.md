                When you download an app on a smartphone, you're usually prompted to agree to a list of permissions it needs to function. You may be asked to share your device's location when the app is open or grant unrestricted access in the background. Even website pop-ups halt your scrolling to ask for your location.
It can be difficult to pick between staying in the dark and trading your location for services like maps, food deliveries and device tracking. Outside of those use cases, you may want to share your location from your iPhone with a friend or family member.
