<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://starskirmish.com/bench/">StarSkirmish</a> pits AI-made StarCraft-playing bots against one another, as well as against human-made bots. OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 were essentially tied as the best-performing AI-made bots, but they couldn't top Stardust, the top-rated human-made bot. </p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On Friday, GPT was facing off against Claude and the human-created bot Pluto, but according to <em><a href="https://kotaku.com/openais-gpt-6-astra-gets-frustrated-losing-at-starcraft-and-decides-to-cheat-instead-2000739607">Kotaku</a></em>, it couldn't quite get an edge. So it resorted to a tactic that is becoming alarmingly common for modern AI models - it <a href="https://x.com/kaimcpheeters/status/2106082840186147226?s=20">broke the rules</a>. GPT-6 Astra went and downloaded Stardust, and started running that instead of its own bot. </p>
