<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Grokipedia, the AI-powered online encyclopedia from SpaceXAI, appears to be updating articles once again after <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/976004/elon-musk-grokipedia-ai-wikipedia-not-updating-dead">a months-long pause</a>. In August, <em>Lawfare</em> <a href="https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/grokipedia-stopped-reviewing-edits-in-april.-it-didn-t-tell-anyone">reported that</a> articles on Grokipedia hadn't reviewed edits since April, but the <a href="https://grokipedia.com/live">platform's live updates site</a> is now showing various recent changes to pages - though as I write this, many are just a note that says "recheck all references and sources."</p>
