                The era of video game adaptations continues. Chernin Entertainment just inked a deal to adapt both Arc Raiders and The Finals as TV and film projects, according to a report by Variety. Embark Studios, who published both games, will also be involved in some capacity.
We don't have any actual details here, other than some corporate press releases that boast about "the next big step in growing" both franchises and how this will offer "integrated transmedia experiences." The president of Chernin did say that these are "ambitious global IPs with the potential to become major franchises across film and television." It sounds like the company is proposing a couple of shared cinematic universes, but that's conjecture on my part.
