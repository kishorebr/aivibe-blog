<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Consulting firm McKinsey &amp; Company is going to ask some entry-level candidates to have a 10-minute conversation with an AI simulation of a client as part of the interview process, <a href="https://www.wsj.com/lifestyle/careers/mckinsey-interview-process-ai-7d08a69e">according to <em>The Wall Street Journal</em></a>.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The AI simulation "aims to reflect 80% of the scenarios McKinsey consultants might encounter in their day-to-day work," the <em>WSJ</em> says, and it isn't designed to "mimic an unhinged client." The AI won't score the candidates - humans will be in the room to assess the candidates and see how well they deal with the simulated situations.</p>
