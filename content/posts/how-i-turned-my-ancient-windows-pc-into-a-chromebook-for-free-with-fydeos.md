    How I turned my ancient Windows PC into a Chromebook for free - with FydeOS
     
    FydeOS aims to bring a Google Chromebook-like experience to standard PCs, and it succeeds - with a caveat or two.
      Written by 
            Jack Wallen, Contributing WriterContributing Writer  Aug. 25, 2026 at 8:37 a.m. PT                            Jack Wallen/ZDNET Follow ZDNET: Add us as a preferred source on Google.ZDNET's key takeawaysFydeOS is a great way to give new life to old hardware.If you like ChromeOS, you'll enjoy FydeOS.FydeOS is free and can be installed on laptops and desktops alike.ChromeOS is the operating system that powers Chromebooks. It's simple, it's useful, and it runs on machines that Windows would cripple. Having ChromeOS for standard PCs would mean that average users could install the OS, bring those old machines back to life, and enjoy a Chromebook experience without having to purchase a Chromebook.FydeOS is a Linux-based OS that looks and feels just like ChromeOS, down to the Material Me design. It includes background updates (so you don't have to worry about remembering to update), seamlessly supports web, Android, and Linux apps, boots in 8 seconds, uses read-only access and "virus armour" for hardened security, FydeOS AI that seamlessly integrates with the OS, and it's free.Also: First look at Googlebook: A premium Chromebook alternative for Android usersI installed FydeOS on an aging laptop to see how it would fare, and, other than one small issue, it came out a winner.Picking the right versionThis is one aspect that FydeOS really needs to change. Choosing the right version of FydeOS can make or break your experience. Choose the wrong version, and your display, network, keyboard, sound, or trackpad may not work. The problem is that the descriptions of the versions aren't exactly user-friendly.
