                AirPods aren't necessarily the best wireless earbuds money can buy, but they should be on your shortlist if you're an Apple fan. They're deeply integrated into the Apple ecosystem, enabling perks like rapid device switching, live translation and seamless sharing with (similarly-equipped) friends.
One of AirPods' highlights is just how good their active noise cancellation (ANC) can be, particularly on the AirPods Max 2 and AirPods Pro 3. In addition, those and a few other models (Pro 2, AirPods 4 with ANC and AirPods 5) offer an enhanced version of ANC called Adaptive Audio. To use this, compatible models must have Apple's custom H2 chip (or better).
