<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Suno announced plans to implement a new watermarking technology and download policy to limit the spread of spammy AI tracks and increase transparency. In a <a href="https://suno.com/blog/building-the-future-of-music-responsibly">lengthy blog post</a>, CEO and co-founder Mikey Shulman laid out the company's principles and the next steps for the company as it seeks legitimacy. </p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The company is rolling out new transparency tools, along with new watermarking and fingerprinting tech. Shulman says it aligns with "<a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/974571/eu-ai-act-transparency-labels-rules-deepfakes">emerging industry standards</a>" that will make it easier to identify Suno-generated content. He also says the company is aiming to partner with "distribution platforms on combatting <a href="https://www.theverge.com/column/921599/ai-music-is-flooding-streaming-services-but-who-wants-it">fraud and misuse</a>." </p>
