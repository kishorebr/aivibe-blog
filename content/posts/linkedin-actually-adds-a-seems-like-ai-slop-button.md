<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A lot of content on LinkedIn might seem like AI slop, and now, you'll be able to report those posts. As part of a series of updates to reduce the volume of AI slop on the platform, LinkedIn is introducing an actual button that lets you flag a post as something that "Seems like AI slop."</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The new feature is part of a broader push to reduce the volume of apparent AI slop on the platform. AI detector Pangram <a href="https://www.pangram.com/blog/ai-in-your-feed?ref=404media.co">recently found</a> that 41 percent of longform LinkedIn posts were flagged as being completely generated by AI, <a href="https://www.404media.co/linkedin-and-x-are-flooded-with-ai-spam-browsing-data-suggests/">as reported by <em>404Media</em></a>.</p>
