                Following a number of complaints about network issues affecting iPhone 18 Pro Max devices, Apple has now confirmed that there is indeed a problem. It appears that the issue has only affected AT&T users in the US, and an iOS update is already available to fix it. That said, the update won't work for those who have already been affected by the problem. Those will need a hardware replacement, Apple told 9to5Mac.
