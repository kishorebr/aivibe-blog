<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Music startup <a href="https://thoughtfulthings.ai/">Thoughtful Things</a> has just launched the Kickstarter campaign for its first instrument, <a href="https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/evmaki/engram-generative-audio-sampler-and-groovebox?ref=section-homepage-view-more-discovery-p1&amp;category_id=Q2F0ZWdvcnktMzM5#use-of-ai">Engram</a>. It's a sampler and groovebox that uses AI to mangle incoming audio and even hallucinate completely new sounds. This isn't Suno in a box, though. This isn't a "push-button, get-song" device, aimed at creating something that sounds ready for top-40 radio. It's about creating experimental, uncanny sounds and pushing AI audio models beyond their limits.</p>
