<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Following this week's midterms where voters resoundingly voiced their <a href="https://www.theverge.com/policy/1026365/data-centers-midterms-results">opposition to data center expansion</a>, OpenAI today announced <a href="https://openai.com/index/setting-new-standard-responsible-ai-infrastructure/">new commitments</a> to communities where it is working on data center projects, including that it won't use non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) with public officials.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Many data center projects have come under scrutiny for relying on NDAs to <a href="https://www.npr.org/2026/08/27/nx-s1-5879528/data-center-nda-disclosure-louisiana">hide discussions</a> between developers and local officials. However, OpenAI says it is now allowing "only narrow protections covering legitimate security information, trade secrets, or proprietary or competitively sensitive information."
