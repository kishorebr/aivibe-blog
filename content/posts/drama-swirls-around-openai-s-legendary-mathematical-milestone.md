<p class="wp-block-paragraph">OpenAI says it found a solution to a major math problem that has remained unsolved for around 90 years, as reported earlier <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/08/science/openai-proof-millennium-problem.html?partner=slack&amp;smid=sl-share">by <em>The New York Times</em></a> and <a href="https://www.wired.com/story/openai-navier-stokes-math-discovery-academics/"><em>Wired</em></a>. In <a href="https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/">a blog post on Tuesday</a>, OpenAI announced that it discovered a solution to the Navier-Stokes problem - which relates to the flow of liquid and gas - using an internal AI model more powerful than the newly released GPT-6 Astra alongside 10,000 concurrent agents. The Navier-Stokes problem is one of seven <a href="https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/">Millennium Prize Problems</a>, each of which comes with a $1 million reward for solving. </p>
