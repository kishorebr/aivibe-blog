                Back at its Dev Day event in September, OpenAI debuted ChatGPT Space. It's a hub that allows you, your coworkers or friends and any Dot agents you've set up to share information and collaborate on documents and other files. On release, ChatGPT Space was available to users on the company's Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. Now, OpenAI is expanding availability to those paying for its $20 per month Plus subscription. If you're one those people, you can create your own Spaces starting today.
You can create your first one by selecting "Space" from the left-hand navigation bar. It's where the Library section used to live. From there, click or tap the plus icon to make your first Space, and select a template or start uploading pages, slides, spreadsheets, images or links to websites.
