<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Meta is planning to price the Muse Charm, a little gadget with a screen that lets you chat with your Muse AI agent, at $300, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/meta-bets-300-muse-charm-boost-ai-hardware-business">according to <em>The Information</em></a>. CEO Mark Zuckerberg revealed the gadget <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/999750/muse-charm-meta-ai-hardware">earlier this year</a> and said the company expected to ship the Charm "in time for the holidays in December," but he didn't provide a price at the time.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When he announced the Muse Charm, Zuckerberg said that the company had "packed the whole Muse experience," including real-time <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028015/meta-muse-ai-voice-mode-expressive-avatar">voice and avatar</a> technology, into the gadget. It has a fingerprint sensor, and you can tap it to be able to talk to a Muse AI agent without unlocking a phone or opening an app. <em> …</em></p>
