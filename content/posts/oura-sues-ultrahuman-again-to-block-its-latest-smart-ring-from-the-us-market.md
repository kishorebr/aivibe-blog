                Last year, Oura successfully sued Ultrahuman and got a court to block sales of its Ring Air product in the US. Now, Oura has sued Ultrahuman again in a Texas federal court over its latest product, the Ring Pro, saying it violates two of its patents involving design and internal components, according to a Oura's blog post. Oura is seeking monetary damages and wants to (again) stop Ultrahuman from selling its latest wearable. 
"After it was found to be infringing Oura patents, Ultrahuman recently introduced a modified version of its ring, claiming its redesign no longer infringed our patents. That claim is false. Ultrahuman's latest ring still incorporates many of Oura's patented innovations and continues to infringe our intellectual property," Oura claimed in the post.
