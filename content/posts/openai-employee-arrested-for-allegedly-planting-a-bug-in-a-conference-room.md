<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An OpenAI engineer was arrested after <a href="https://x.com/OpenAINewsroom/status/2110095403198849132?s=20">the company said it discovered</a> a "sophisticated recording device" hidden in one of its offices. The employee was identified as Abdulrahman Alfozan, a 35-year-old Saudi national. Alfozan is charged with using or endeavoring to use a device to intercept oral communications, <a href="https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/saudi-national-arrested-installing-surreptitious-audio-recording-device-openai">according to a press release from the Department of Justice</a>.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">OpenAI's post about the incident says it used its AI models to "understand the device and where it had come from, identifying that a specific employee had planted it a few days earlier."</p>
