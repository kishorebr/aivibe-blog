                YouTube view counts are about to go up (again) thanks to a new metric. Google's video streaming platform may count more than one person when videos are viewed on a TV, according to an X post from YouTube Creator Insider. These extra "co-views" will apply to long form, Shorts, and Live videos, for TV views only. 
YouTube thinks that is has been undercounting viewers in group-watching situations, thus not giving creators a true picture of a video's reach. The goal with the new metric, the platform wrote, is to "[align] with TV industry standards — including how traditional broadcast and streaming networks estimate the total number of people exposed to content."
