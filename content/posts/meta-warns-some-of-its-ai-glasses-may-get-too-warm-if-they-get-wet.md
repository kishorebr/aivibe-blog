                Meta is rolling out a software update for its AI glasses to fix an issue that causes some devices to become "too warm" when they get wet. The company describes it as a "rare issue" affecting only a "small number" of its glasses, but is nonetheless urging everyone with a pair of Meta Glasses to update their software and offering free replacements to those affected.
The company has begun notifying users about the forthcoming update which will roll out to everyone with a pair of Meta-branded frames, which includes Adventurer, Fury, Starfire, Nova and Capri models. Oakley and Ray-Ban glasses are not impacted, according to Meta.
