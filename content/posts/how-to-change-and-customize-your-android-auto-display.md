                Most drivers stick with Android Auto's default setup from the moment they connect their phone, never realizing just how much of the display can be reshaped to fit their habits. From rearranging your layout to building one-tap shortcuts, Android Auto gives you more control over your dashboard than its stock appearance suggests.
The display of Android Auto can be tailored to match your driving habits perfectly without even having to dip into any hidden menus. With options to rearrange icons, create custom shortcuts and adjust visual elements, it allows you to design a dashboard that feels intuitive and personal. Whether you prioritize navigation, music or communication, every feature can be optimized for seamless access, making your driving experience smoother and more efficient.
Every adjustment takes just a minute or two, but together they add up to a noticeably smoother, safer and more personal driving experience.
