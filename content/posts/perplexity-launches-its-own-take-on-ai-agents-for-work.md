<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Perplexity is taking on OpenAI and Anthropic with its own work-focused AI agents. The startup's new always-on "Teammates" can take on multiple roles within a workspace, while pulling existing projects, skills, and memory from the Perplexity tools you already use.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Teammates integrate with <a href="https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-perplexity-computer">Perplexity Computer</a>, an AI system that can deploy agents on your device to complete tasks. But Perplexity says Teammates "build on top" of Computer by offering the ability to collaborate and complete "ongoing, large-scale work." You can assign each Teammate different jobs that they can perform simultaneously - much <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004096/openai-chatgpt-dots-hands-on-agent">like OpenAI's Dots</a> and <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1015843/anthropic-claude-duet-ai-agent">Claude Duet</a>.
