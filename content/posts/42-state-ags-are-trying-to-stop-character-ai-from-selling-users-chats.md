<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A coalition of <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/state-ags-fight-character-ais-plan-to-auction-off-users-private-chats/">42 state attorneys general </a>is <a href="https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/characterai.pdf">trying to prevent</a> Character.AI from <a href="https://www.theverge.com/policy/1011417/new-york-ag-characterai-bankruptcy">selling off user data</a> as it navigates Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings. In a <a href="https://oag.maryland.gov/News/pages/Attorney-General-Brown-Challenges-Plan-to-Auction-Off-Character.AI-Users%E2%80%99-Private-Chats%2C-Including-Kids%E2%80%99-Data%2C-to-Highest-B.aspx">deluge</a> of <a href="https://portal.ct.gov/ag/press-releases/2026-press-releases/attorney-general-tong-challenges-plan-to-auction-off-character-ai-private-chats">nearly</a> <a href="https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-opposes-asset-sale-characterai-amid-child-safety-concerns">identical</a> <a href="https://www.ag.state.mn.us/Office/Communications/2026/11/23_CharacterAI.asp">press</a> releases, the various AGs accuse the company of planning to auction off "the private chat histories of more than 21 million U.S. accounts - more than 2.1 million of them registered to minors - with no safeguards for the people whose conversations are being sold."</p>
