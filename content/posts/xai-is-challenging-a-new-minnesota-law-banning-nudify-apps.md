                xAI — now officially known as SpaceXAI after the merging of Elon Musk's two companies – has filed a lawsuit against Minnesota Attorney General Keith Ellison to challenge the state's new law that would ban apps and websites that can generate nonconsensual intimate images. It would also impose fines on their developers every time people use them to create sexual deepfakes. The law, the first of its kind in the nation, was approved by Gov. Tim Walz earlier this year and is slated to take effect in August. 
In its complaint, the company says the law was an "overbroad, content-based ban on free speech and the tools of visual expression in a clumsy attempt to prohibit 'nudification.'" xAI, it reads, isn't contesting Minnesota's "interest in prohibiting the dissemination of artificially generated nude images of real people without their consent." However, the law "extends far beyond that goal, exposing a wide array of protected speech to civil liability and government sanction."
