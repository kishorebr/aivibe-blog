                Huawei Technologies has been found guilty of bank fraud and conspiracy to steal trade secrets by a US jury, the Associated Press reports. The verdict is the result of charges brought by the Department of Justice in 2019, though the jury acquitted Huawei of racketeering conspiracy, as well as conspiracy to obstruct justice.
Racketeering is traditionally reserved for organized criminal acts — the kind often associated with the Mafia, for example — but the AP writes US prosecutors thought the charge could apply to what they described as Huawei's repeated theft of intellectual property and trade secrets. The company has been accused of copying or outright stealing from competitors in the past, and the DOJ pointed to Huawei's sale of routers with code copied from a competitor as evidence.
