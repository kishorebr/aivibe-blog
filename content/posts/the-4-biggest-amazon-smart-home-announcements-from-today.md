ZDNET’s key takeaways




Amazon just announced five new smart home devices for its lineup.



There’s new categories: the $230 Alexa Photo Frame and the $200 Echo Time.



And new Alexa Home Map creates 3D maps of your home’s interior and exterior.








Amazon announced several new smart home devices today, including two all-new device categories: the $200 Echo Time smart alarm clock and the $230 Alexa Photo Frame. Both are available now, along with an upgraded Echo Dot for $100 and a new Echo Studio with a built-in Home Camera for $250.



Also: Amazon’s new Echo Dot can turn into a Wi-Fi extender – how it works



The company’s smart home lineup also now includes a redesigned $500 Echo Show 16, which is available for preorder, with devices shipping in mid-December. Finally, Amazon announced the Alexa Home Map, which lets you create a 3D interactive map of your home’s interior and exterior.
