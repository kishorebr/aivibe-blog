Dreamteam, a startup founded by former Freshworks executives, has raised $53 million to challenge Salesforce and HubSpot with its AI-native CRM, betting that AI can finally shake up a market where established players have so far proved difficult to dislodge.

The funding comprises seed and Series A rounds, led by Together Fund and Accel respectively, with participation from Premji Invest. The San Francisco-based startup declined to disclose the size of each round or its valuation.



Traditional CRMs depend on salespeople to manually record customer interactions and keep deal information up to date. That often leaves businesses with incomplete or outdated information. Dreamteam aims to eliminate much of that work by automatically capturing context from sources including emails and customer conversations. It also uses AI agents to handle tasks such as follow-ups, meeting preparation, and pipeline monitoring.

Dreamteam co-founder and CEO Anand Venkatraman (pictured above, right) told TechCrunch that he had repeatedly encountered problems with traditional CRM platforms over more than 25 years in enterprise software, including at Salesforce and Freshworks. Salespeople spent considerable time updating records, yet managers still struggled to get a reliable picture of their business. “In all these years, I have never come across an instance where I could click a button and get a forecast from the CRM,” he said.

That experience led Venkatraman to co-found Dreamteam in 2025 with Senthil Kanthaswamy (pictured above, center), who spent more than nine years at Freshworks, most recently as a product leader at Freshservice, and Ramesh Parthasarathy (pictured above, left), who previously worked on AI and platform engineering at Freshworks.
