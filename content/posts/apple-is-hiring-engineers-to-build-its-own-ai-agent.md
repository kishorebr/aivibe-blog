<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Apple is working on its own AI agent that can perform a range of tasks for you, including browsing the web, working across apps, writing code, and calling services. That's according to <a href="https://jobs.apple.com/en-us/details/200695488-0836/senior-engineering-manager-agentic-ai-proactive?team=SFTWR">a job listing</a> spotted <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/apple-is-working-on-ai-agent-for-device-control-apps-browsing-and-coding">by <em>Bloomberg</em></a>, which says Apple is looking for an engineer to lead the creation of an agentic AI assistant.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An AI agent from Apple would put the iPhone maker in the running to compete with the likes of <a href="https://www.theverge.com/column/999999/optimizer-meta-muse-ai-cute">Meta's Muse</a>, <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1002033/openai-dots-launch-muse-competitor">OpenAI's Dots</a>, <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032518/claude-duet-ai-agent-hands-on">Anthropic's Duet</a>, and <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/1035756/google-spark-ai-tasks-free">Google's Tasks</a>. Muse, ChatGPT, and Perplexity can already take control of a computer through their Mac apps, while <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/1037902/google-gemini-macos-ai-computer-use">Google just started letting Gemini</a> do the same.</p>
