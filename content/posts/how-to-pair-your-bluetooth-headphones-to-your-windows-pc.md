                Bluetooth headphones and earbuds are now incredibly common, and more people than ever own a pair. But despite how long Bluetooth has been around, pairing Bluetooth headphones to a computer can still be fiddly. Microsoft hasn't helped, either, with Windows 11's Bluetooth stability a constant bugbear, too, making things even worse — but more on that later.
On paper, connecting headphones to a Windows 11 computer is relatively straightforward. The simplest way is to click the speaker icon in the bottom-right of the taskbar to open the quick settings panel, click the arrow next to the Bluetooth icon, and make sure the Bluetooth toggle is set to the on position.
