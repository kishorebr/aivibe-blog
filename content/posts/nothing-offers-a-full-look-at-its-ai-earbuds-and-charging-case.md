<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Nothing <a href="https://x.com/nothing/status/2125590327189021093?a=Su5GIj_FqQDB3ddRSgE&amp;s=20">is showing off a much more detailed look</a> at its upcoming AI earbuds ahead of their December 8th release. The Ear Note buds can record your calls, while their charging case can record, transcribe, and summarize your conversations.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/792839/nothing-ear-3-review-audio-super-mic-price">Like the Ear 3</a>, the Ear Note's microphone-equipped case comes with a "Talk" button that you can use to record your own voice. There's also a new "Record" button that can record and transcribe your conversations. <a href="https://x.com/getpeid/status/2125596507307180044?a=F_huihKP6YwdY81UzAc&amp;s=20">In a post on X</a>, Nothing CEO Carl Pei says the button will allow you to record calls through the earbuds, and meetings through the case as a "first step toward a hardware context layer for AI."</p>
