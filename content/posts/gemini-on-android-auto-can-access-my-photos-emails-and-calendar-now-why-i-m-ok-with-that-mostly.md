ZDNET’s key takeaways




Google is rolling out Gemini Personal Intelligence on Android Auto starting today.



This gives the AI assistant access to your Photos, Gmail, Calendar, and more.



It’s an opt-in feature, and you can select which apps and services to permit access to.








It was only a matter of time. After rolling out Gemini across Android Auto and cars with Google built-in late last year, the Mountain View giant is now launching phase two of its AI world domination. Sorry, I mean… launching enhancements to the AI assistant to help you live more productively.



Also: 6 Android Auto apps I wish I discovered sooner



Starting today, you may grant Gemini on Android Auto access to your Gmail, Calendar, Photos, and other native services to provide tailored responses. It’s Personal Intelligence, but for your car.
