                Another OpenAI employee tasked with ensuring the safety of its models has left. Following his departure, David Robinson, who used to lead the writing of safety reports that were published with model launches, wrote an article for The Atlantic that warned of a "broken" company culture. Among the many points warning of the dangers associated with the trajectory that AI development is on, the former safety lead explained that "as the company springs from one launch to the next, it is failing to achieve the level of care that I believe is needed."
