                If you're a frequent traveler, you may frequently encounter signage or other text in foreign languages. That's all well and good if you have enough knowledge to piece together a translation, but if you can't even understand the alphabet in question, you're faced with a much bigger challenge.
The good news is that your phone's camera can recognize and translate just about any text in seconds, even if the language uses an entirely different script. While a translation app may seem like the obvious choice for this task, it's actually not always your best bet. Depending on the languages involved and the context, you may sometimes need more than one tool. Luckily, your phone's camera is all you need to translate pretty much anything in the real world.
