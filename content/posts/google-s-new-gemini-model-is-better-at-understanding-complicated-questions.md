<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Google says its new <a href="https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/gemini-4-hydrogen-search-upgrade/">Gemini 4 Hydrogen</a> model can give more helpful answers to complex questions, including "questions with multiple parts, hard-to-understand typos, or highly specific requests." The new model can break down your questions and address each part in its responses. It's <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/">available starting today</a> in search, the Gemini app, all tiers of Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, and in Google Antigravity for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. Robby Stein, VP of product at Google Search, <a href="https://x.com/rmstein/status/2120941443359576166?a=lerZsroBaoIBT9jNz00&amp;s=20">says</a> it's "now the new default model in AI Mode &amp; being used for your hardest questions in AI Overviews."</p>
