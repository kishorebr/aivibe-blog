                Uber has grand ambitions for its drone delivery program. It's aiming to reach 1 million drone deliveries per day by 2029 after partnering with Zipline. The service is kicking off today, as Uber Eats customers in the Houston suburb of Spring, Texas, can now request drone deliveries. Dallas is the next market ahead of a broader expansion to dozens of cities across the US.
Zipline's drones can deliver orders weighing up to eight pounds as far as 10 miles away from a restaurant. Uber said the drones can fly in rain and other bad weather at up to 70 mph. They lower pods containing orders into customers' yards or driveways from more than 300 feet in the air. Uber added that the drones take the quietest paths to their destinations, depending on the wind, speed and altitude.
