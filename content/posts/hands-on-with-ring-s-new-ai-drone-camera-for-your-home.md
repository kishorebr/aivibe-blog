<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ring is finally shipping a flying indoor drone camera. For real this time. The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H1PBKM3X">Ring Drone Cam</a> will arrive on <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H1PBKM3X">January 27th, 2027, for $249.99</a>, Ring founder Jamie Siminoff said at a launch event in New York City this week. If it does actually ship, it will arrive almost six years after the company announced its first flying camera, <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2021/9/28/22692048/ring-always-home-cam-drone-amazon-price-release-date-specs">the Ring Always Home Cam.</a></p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Designed to patrol inside your home when you're not there, the Drone Cam is an AI-powered drone that can react to triggers from Ring sensors, such as detecting motion, a door opening, a water leak, or an alarm sounding.
