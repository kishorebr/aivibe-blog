<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Signal is restricting the ability to take screenshots on Android and iOS devices in an effort to prevent AI agents from reading users' messages and other data. The agentic AI capabilities built into operating systems that analyze on-screen information to learn more about the user are "undermining Signal's ability to independently guarantee privacy," Signal president <a href="https://signal.org/blog/protecting-your-privacy/">Meredith Whittaker said in a blog post</a>, while urging OS providers to make it easier for app developers to control what data agents can access.</p>
