<p class="has-drop-cap wp-block-paragraph">Instagram's visible AI labels are supposed to help people quickly spot synthetically generated content at a glance. They say Meta has been automatically applying an "AI Content" label to images that they didn't create or edit using generative AI tools. Meanwhile, actual AI imagery is slipping through the cracks, leaving the impression that nothing can be trusted on Instagram at all.</p>
