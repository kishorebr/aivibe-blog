                We are less than a month away from the premiere of a brand new show in the Avatar-verse, which is happening 12 full years after The Legend of Korra ended its run. Avatar: Seven Havens starts streaming via Paramount+ on October 9 and we finally have a real beefy trailer to get us pumped.
We got some footage back in July, but that was more of a teaser. This is nearly three minutes of the show, complete with character voices and plenty of hints as to where the story is going. The animation also looks very nice, which is typical for the franchise.

The story looks steeped in post-apocalyptic flavor. Something bad happened after the events of The Legend of Korra and the world is in shambles, forcing survivors to retreat to the titular seven havens. As a matter of fact, Korra is being blamed for these events (which is on brand) so public sentiment has shifted negatively toward the entire concept of Avatars.
