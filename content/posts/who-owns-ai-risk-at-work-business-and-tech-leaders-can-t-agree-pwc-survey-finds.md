But we’re now experiencing the trade-offs. Rogue AI models, friendly AI hacking innocent companies, potentially thousands of AI-related security incidents being investigated, and AI agents acting as new entry points into corporate networks. 



Who should be responsible and take ownership of AI when things go wrong? According to new research from PwC, businesses can’t agree. 



Cybersecurity and AI in the boardroom



On Friday, PwC released its Digital Trust Insights 2027 report, which surveyed approximately 4,000 business and tech leaders across 71 countries.



According to the survey, no single role has a clear responsibility for managing agentic AI or its security, although awareness of both the benefits and disadvantages of AI has reached the board level in around half of businesses.
