                Many iPad problems can be fixed with a simple restart, but sometimes that's not enough. Severe issues with the tablet's software can make it completely inoperable, and in these cases, putting the iPad in recovery mode might be your only option. 
Recovery mode also lets you connect your iPad to your computer to update it, or in the worst-case scenario, restore it to its factory default settings. It's best to save recovery mode for when nothing else works, but when it's needed, it can mean the difference between a bricked iPad and one that works just like new.
There are a few situations when you might want to use recovery mode. Major software updates can sometimes fail, leaving you with a partially installed update that keeps your iPad from turning on.
