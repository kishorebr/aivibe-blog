                NVIDIA has reportedly put a stop to shipments of AI server hardware to four Southeast Asian countries. According to The Financial Times, a handful of the company's manufacturing partners, including Dell and Supermicro, told clients in Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia that the chipmaker is not allowing their server orders to be sent to them. The Financial Times reports it's unclear if the halt is temporary. The news comes just days after NVIDIA reinstated Jensen Huang as its chief executive.
In recent years, Chinese AI labs have turned to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia to gain access to NVIDIA's most powerful chips. They used a gap in US export controls by renting access through cloud compute providers that bought servers with NVIDIA GPUs inside.
