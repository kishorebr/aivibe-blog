                Tidal will soon become the first streaming service to take a decisive stance against AI-generated music. On Tuesday, the company announced it would begin removing wholly genAI tracks from its catalog come December 9. According to platform lead Robert Anderson, Tidal's decision will result in the deletion of approximately 29 million tracks, and set the service on the path to reject 80,000 to 85,000 songs daily. "We believe this will resonate with both artists and listeners alike, as 96 percent of all AI-generated songs receive zero plays at all," Anderson wrote on X. "We are the first DSP to do so, and we encourage our peers to follow suit."
