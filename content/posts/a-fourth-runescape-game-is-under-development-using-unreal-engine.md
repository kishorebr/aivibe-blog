                The fourth major installment in the RuneScape world is coming and it's being developed using Unreal Engine. Jagex announced the next game during RuneFest, its annual convention for all things RuneScape, with a working title of RS4 and an accompanying trailer.
While the minute-long video doesn't reveal much about the latest MMO, it looks like players will be able to ride and fight dragons. RS4 will add to Jagex's roster of RuneScape games, including RuneScape, Old School RuneScape and RuneScape: Dragonwilds. Notably, Jagex said that the upcoming title isn't meant to replace any of the older titles, explaining that it's "designing RS4 to sit alongside them as an additional way to experience RuneScape and the world of Gielinor."
