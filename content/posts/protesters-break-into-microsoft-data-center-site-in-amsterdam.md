<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Protesters have broken into a construction site of a new Microsoft data center in Amsterdam this morning. Members of the Geef Tegengas group helped organize the protest against the construction of three 85-meter <a href="https://puredc.com/amsterdam">data center towers</a> in the outskirts of Amsterdam.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/protesters-enter-microsoft-data-centre-site-amsterdam-2026-11-06/"><em>Reuters </em>reports</a> that around 100 people made it into the construction site, with some scaling a crane to place a "fuck AI" banner. Protesters also unfurled a "Microsoft powers genocide" banner on the construction site, and chanted "Microsoft has got to go" and "free Palestine."</p>
