<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) is creating <a href="https://www.a16z.news/p/introducing-the-horowitz-andreessen">an "academy" positioned</a> as a pipeline for young people to build or join a Silicon Valley startup. The "Horowitz Andreessen Academy" will launch with 10 partners, including Anduril, Anthropic, Coinbase, Google, Meta, Nvidia, OpenAI, Palantir, Replit, and Stripe, along with $42 million in funding led by a16z. While billed as a "highly selective school," it does not grant any degree or accreditation. Instead, students will attend short classes led by tech figureheads, like OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, as well as "co-ops" that give students roles at tech companies.</p>
