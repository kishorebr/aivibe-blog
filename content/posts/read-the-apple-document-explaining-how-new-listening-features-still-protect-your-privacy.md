<p class="wp-block-paragraph">At Wednesday's <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tech/991203/iphone-duo-foldable-news-specs-price-release-date">iPhone Duo launch event</a>, Apple announced a handful of new Siri AI <a href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/148354" data-type="link" data-id="https://support.apple.com/en-us/148354?cid=general-watch-b3d8fc66-62020270">Audio Intelligence features</a>, including Siri Recap, Live Rewind, Sound Recognition, and Music Recognition. Alongside its announcement, Apple <a href="https://www.apple.com/privacy/docs/Audio_Intelligence_Privacy_Overview_Sep_2026.pdf">released a document</a> laying out how it plans to balance AI "ambient listening" and users' privacy. It says the raw audio from the new features "is handled within dedicated hardware, is not saved as a file, and is not accessible to the operating system, apps, or Apple."</p>
