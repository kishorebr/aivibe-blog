                The lack of Face ID is one of the main complaints users have had about the iPhone Duo. And while rumors suggest that the company might add a TrueDepth camera to the second generation of its foldable phone next year, it seems that Apple has a more immediate solution to address these complaints. The second beta of iOS 27.3, released to developers on Monday, adds a new option to unlock the iPhone Duo with facial recognition using its front-facing camera.
The feature is called "Unlock with Front Camera" and can be enabled in the Touch ID & Passcode settings in the Settings app. Engadget confirmed that the feature works on an iPhone Duo running the beta — but how it works is quite intriguing. Unlike most modern iPhones, the Duo lacks a TrueDepth camera with the depth sensors that Face ID uses to map a face in 3D.
