<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Meta's new terms appear to give it permission to crawl and scrape websites "associated" with the businesses that use its platforms - including online publishers, according to <a href="https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/10/metas-new-commercial-terms-will-allow-the-scraping-of-news-sites-legal-experts-say/">a report from <em>Nieman Lab</em></a>. The <a href="https://www.facebook.com/legal/commercial_terms_preview">new terms</a>, which go into effect on October 30th, say Meta can use this outside content to "develop, improve, research," and "train" its AI products.</p>
