<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Suno has introduced image and video generation in its latest effort to expand beyond being an AI music platform. The new feature, <a href="https://suno.com/blog/introducing-visuals">called "Visuals,"</a> allows users to turn Suno songs into AI-generated video clips that automatically lipsync to lyrics, or make images via prompt descriptions that can be used as thumbnails and album artwork.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Visuals is available on web, iOS, and Android. The exact layout differs across Suno's desktop and mobile interfaces, but users will find <a href="https://suno.com/visuals">a new Visuals tab under Create mode</a> on each version.
