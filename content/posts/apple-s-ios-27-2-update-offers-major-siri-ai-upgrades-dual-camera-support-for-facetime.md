ZDNET’s key takeaways




The new iOS 27.2 helps Siri AI better recall details from past chats.



Plus, you can now use the front and rear cameras together on a FaceTime call.



Apple also updated the iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and other devices.








Apple has kicked off its first major update for iOS 27 with the release of iOS 27.2 on Monday. Though the new version adds several intriguing changes, the ones for Siri AI may be the most helpful.



With this latest update nestled on your iPhone, Siri AI can now recall your previous conversations. That means you can ask it about topics you’ve discussed in the past, and it should remember what it told you and pick up on that in a new chat.
