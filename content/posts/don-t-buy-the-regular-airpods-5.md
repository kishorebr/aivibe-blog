                Before yesterday, I wasn't expecting new AirPods during the iPhone Duo event. When Bloomberg finally committed to an AirPods 5 prediction the morning of the show, I knew the updates would be minor at best. For various reasons, including the lack of spicy rumors, it just didn't seem like Apple had anything groundbreaking to add to its base-level AirPods lineup. As it turns out, it didn't.
I will concede that the upgrades to the model that Apple is now calling AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case are notable. And doing so for $149 when the comparable AirPods 4 model was $179 is commendable. I already know how much the swipe gestures for volume control will improve the experience. I say this as someone who constantly tries to swipe the stems of the AirPods 4 every time I wear them. I've definitely been AirPods Pro-pilled there. Live Translation is also a substantial addition, no doubt about that.
