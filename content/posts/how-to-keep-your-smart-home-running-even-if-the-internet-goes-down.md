                With Apple finally developing new smart home products, now's a great time to remind everyone that though the technology can improve your home's look and convenience, there are also a few hazards involved. If you're overly ambitious with your routines, for example, smart bulbs might switch off when people need them. Once, while we installed a smart thermostat, the heat ran dangerously out of control until we fixed an obscure flaw.
For many of you, the biggest issue is going to be offline resilience. When internet access goes down, some smart home accessories can go "dumb," either maintaining their last state or failing to work at all. Usually, this is just an inconvenience. If you're counting on an accessory for health or security, however, this can become a legitimate crisis.
