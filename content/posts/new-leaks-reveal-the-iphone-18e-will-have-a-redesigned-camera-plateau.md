                Apple seems to have adopted a new strategy for the iPhone launch, splitting it into two phases. After launching the premium iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo in September, Apple is preparing new versions of the lower-priced models — and it looks like they'll look a lot more like the more expensive iPhones. CAD images of the new iPhone 18e seen by Engadget show a redesigned camera bump on the back of the phone, similar to the one on the Pro models. The images come from a source familiar with Apple's supply chain who who had previously shared accurate tips on the colors of the iPhone 17 and iPhone 18 Pro before they were announced.
