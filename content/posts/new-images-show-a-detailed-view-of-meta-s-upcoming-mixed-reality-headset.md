                Meta appears to have unintentionally shown off its upcoming mixed reality headset a bit ahead of schedule. Images of the device, which has been known as "Project Phoenix", have surfaced and they provide a fairly detailed look at the lightweight headset.
The images were originally published by UploadVR, which found the files in a HorizonOS app Meta released called "HorizonOS Prescription Lens." The app, which has since been removed, seems to be intended for people who need prescription inserts for the device. But while Meta seems to have released the app as a kind of "placeholder," UploadVR found that it included image files that clearly show the headset from different angles.
