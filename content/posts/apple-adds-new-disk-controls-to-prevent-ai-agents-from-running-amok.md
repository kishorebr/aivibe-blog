                After sounding an alarm about the issue last month, Apple is taking steps to limit Mac disk access to AI agents, the company announced on its developer blog. In its latest macOS beta, Apple introduced controls to ensure that users will need to take "very explicit" actions to grant apps like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini "this extraordinary level of [full disk] access." 
The issue rose to prominence in September, when Inc. columnist Jason Aten noticed that Meta's Muse Mac app was able to access his messages even though he thought he had denied that permission.
