                For years, getting Starlink meant buying whatever satellite dish the company was selling at the time, regardless of your needs. That's no longer the case. Starlink now offers multiple options designed for everything from connecting a rural home to keeping an RV, boat or remote worksite online.
Choosing a Starlink dish isn't exactly difficult, but it's important to understand that the cheapest option, while appealing, won't always be the best. Alternatively, the most expensive option can be overkill. Portability, durability, performance and whether you need internet at a fixed location or on the move can all affect which setup makes sense for you.
There are six Starlink dishes total that we'll cover here, some available in slightly different versions or bundles.
