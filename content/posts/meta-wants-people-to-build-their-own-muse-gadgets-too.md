                We still don't know too much about Meta's tamagotchi-like AI device, but the Muse Charm won't be the only dedicated hardware for the AI agent. The company is now encouraging developers to come up with their own Muse-powered devices with a new open source project called Muse Gadgets. It's setting an example as well, kicking things off with a smart home gadget of its own called the Muse Home Link.
According to the head of product for Meta's superintelligence labs Nat Friedman, Meta is providing the open source firmware and a Linux SDK that will enable people to bring the company's agent to hardware. Meta has made some suggestions as to what form these gadgets could take, including a dedicated E Ink display, an HDMI dongle for your TV or a small touchscreen-enabled accessory that looks a bit like the Muse Charm itself.
