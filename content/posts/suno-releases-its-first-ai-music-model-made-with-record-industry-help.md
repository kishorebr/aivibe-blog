<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Suno's new v6 <a href="https://suno.com/blog/introducing-v6">AI music model</a> is its first made with support from the record industry. Suno's Jack Brody told <em>The Verge</em> that v6 was "trained from the ground up, with a new set of data that does not include the same data that our previous models were trained on." The data includes content licensed from partners <a href="https://www.theverge.com/news/829189/warner-music-group-suno-ai-licensing-deal">Warner Music Group</a>, <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/978232/suno-has-struck-a-global-alliance-with-the-music-company-bmg">BMG</a>, and <a href="https://suno.com/blog/believe-partnership">Believe</a>, as well as "<a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/906896/sunos-copyright-ai-music-covers">user data</a>." It's unclear whether that means v6 training data is completely free of <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/966072/suno-ai-music-training-scraping-youtube-hack">dubiously obtained content</a>.</p>
