<p class="wp-block-paragraph">BBC's <em><a href="https://www.bbcearth.com/shows/blue-planet-iii">Blue Planet III</a></em> is looking to prove its authenticity by leaving in the less-than-perfect shots. Previous incarnations of the nature series were defined by their gorgeous, crystal-clear footage that seemed almost too good to be real. But one dead giveaway of AI is when things look too polished and seamless. To counter that, Blue Planet III goes warts and all.</p>
