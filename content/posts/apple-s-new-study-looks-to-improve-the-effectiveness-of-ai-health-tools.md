<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Apple announced a new study today with the goal of better understanding how people interact with conversational AI health tools and how useful their responses actually are. The company is conducting the Apple Health AI Usage Study in collaboration with <a href="https://www.openevidence.com/">OpenEvidence</a>, a clinical AI platform used by healthcare professionals that generates answers based on medical information from sources like <em>The New England Journal of Medicine</em>. Participants will use a new iOS app to ask medical questions and then rate the responses based on criteria including accuracy, detail, and relevance.</p>
