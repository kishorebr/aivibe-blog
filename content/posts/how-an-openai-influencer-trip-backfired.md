<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The brand trip is a right of passage for influencers. It's a mark of legitimacy that a sponsor wants to invite them on an all-expenses-paid vacation, often with luxurious freebies and activities. Trips can also spur hard feelings from uninvited influencers, trigger criticism from the public, and project a certain frivolousness. </p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Usually it is <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2023/6/30/23779058/shein-influencer-trip-backlash-ads-forced-labor">fast fashion companies</a> or <a href="https://www.thecut.com/2023/05/why-black-women-creators-are-accusing-tarte-of-mistreatment.html">beauty brands</a> flying out influencers with the goal of flooding social media with sponsored content. This time, it was a major AI company.</p>
