<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Anthropic is using AI to build more powerful AI. And it appears to be working remarkably well, with the company's models improving roughly twice as quickly as a year ago, with little indication of slowing down.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">That's according to a new <a href="https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/">report</a> published Thursday by the AI <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/996563/ai-safety-research-metr-redwood-openai-anthropic">safety research organization</a> METR, following an 11-week investigation inside the company, as part of a report evaluating its AI research and development. The probe is separate from a <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1012308/anthropic-metr-employee-like-access">program</a> offering safety researchers "employee-like" access to systems in the wake of <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994064/anthropic-spent-this-week-in-hot-water-over-cybersecurity">high-profile</a> <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1000644/irregular-rogue-ai-cyberattacks-hacking-openai-meta-anthropic-google">hacking</a> <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/985385/openais-rogue-ai-model-hugging-face-cybersecurity-incident-reports-metr">incidents</a> over the summer.</p>
