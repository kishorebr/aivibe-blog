<p class="wp-block-paragraph"> Armed with a team of AI agents, Google researcher Pietro Miotti claimed earlier this week to have discovered a potentially unmapped Maya site in Mexico. There was just one problem: The site had already been discovered and was published in an academic journal last year. The incident, which <a href="https://bsky.app/profile/indyfromspace.bsky.social/post/3n2235hwwsk2m">sparked criticism</a> among archeologists online, underscores a growing tension in science as amateurs wielding increasingly powerful AI tools parachute into specialist fields, often without the expertise needed to understand what they are looking at.</p>
