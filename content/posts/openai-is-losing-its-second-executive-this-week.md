<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Another OpenAI executive is departing. Denise Dresser, who joined OpenAI as its chief revenue officer <a href="https://www.theverge.com/news/841540/openai-slack-ceo-denise-dresser-chief-revenue-officer">in December</a> after serving as CEO of Slack, will be leaving in the "coming weeks" to "pursue other opportunities," she said in <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7493699644359630848/">a team note posted to LinkedIn</a>. Dali Rajic, president and COO of Wiz, will be taking over the CRO role, <a href="https://openai.com/index/dali-rajic-chief-revenue-officer/">OpenAI says</a>.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dresser's departure is just the latest in a string of major exits. (Dresser had taken over some of Lightcap's work when he moved to the special projects role.)
