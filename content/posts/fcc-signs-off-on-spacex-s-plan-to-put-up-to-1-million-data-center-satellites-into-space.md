<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In <a href="https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-427572A1.pdf">an announcement on Wednesday</a>, the FCC says it authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million AI chip-equipped satellites as part of the Elon Musk-owned <a href="https://www.spacex.com/spacexsi/starmind">company's "Starmind" initiative</a>. With <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/845453/space-data-centers-astronomers">several others also pursuing the idea of putting AI data centers in space</a>, including Google and Blue Origin, this is the first approval the agency has granted for a full "constellation" of AI satellites.</p>
