It extends a run of breakthroughs that have both impressed and unsettled parts of the mathematical community while raising questions about research ethics and academic conduct. According to AGMAI, the <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/999167/openai-elite-mathematicians-panel" target="_blank" rel="noopener">newly formed independent advisory group of elite mathematicians</a> put together to work on communicating the results responsibly, the release includes solutions to "hundreds" of open questions.</p>
