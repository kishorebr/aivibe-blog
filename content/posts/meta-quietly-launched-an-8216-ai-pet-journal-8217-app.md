<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Meta has launched a "tamagotchi-style" pet game called Waddle World, with virtual creatures that chat with users and create doodles to illustrate conversations.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">According to its <a href="https://apps.apple.com/ph/app/waddle-world-ai-pet-journal/id6809943562">Apple</a> and <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.meta.waddle&amp;hl=en_GB">Play</a> Store listings, the "16-bit pet care game" is not really a game, but "a daily journal disguised as a pet." Users should talk, not type, with their pets, the description says, as well as play "cozy games" like stacking groceries or word games. The Play store description says:</p>
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A little friend that listens. A cozy world that's yours. Waddle World is a cozy 16-bit world where your pixel pet lives. Hatch a virtual pet, visit whenever you like, and let it …</p></blockquote>
