                Harvey Jones, NVIDIA's recently appointed board chair, joined the company's Q3 earnings call on Tuesday to comment on the resignation of Jensen Huang. Despite the former CEO floating the possibility of a potential return to his old position, Harvey quashed the possibility, saying the board was not considering reinstating Huang. Jones later said he and Colette Kress, the company's new interim CEO, would not answer any questions related to Huang.   
"On November 12th, our board of directors announced that after disclosure of personal behavior inconsistent with the company's code of conduct, standards, and cultural norms, Jensen Huang voluntarily resigned his positions as president, CEO, and board member," Jones said. "The NVIDIA board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO."
