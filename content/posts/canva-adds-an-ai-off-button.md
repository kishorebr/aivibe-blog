<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Canva will now allow you to turn off its generative AI features. <a href="https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/ai-controls-canva/">With this change</a>, you can disable the graphic design platform's array of AI tools, such as its <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2023/10/4/23902794/canva-magic-studio-ai-design-new-tools">AI photo-extending Magic Expand</a> feature, as well as turn off the ability to generate images <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1017151/canva-craft-in-house-ai-model">with its Canva chatbot</a>.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This toggle doesn't turn off <em>every</em> AI-powered feature on Canva, however. That includes Canva's background remover and its Magic Grab tool, which lets you select an object in an image and move it somewhere else. Canva says these tools will stay on because they "make everyday tasks faster without generating anything new."</p>
