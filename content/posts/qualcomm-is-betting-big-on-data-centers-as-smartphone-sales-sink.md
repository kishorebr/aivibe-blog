During its <a href="https://investor.qualcomm.com/files/content_files/FY2026-4th-Quarter-Earnings-Presentation_Final.pdf">Q4 2026 earnings</a> call, the company laid out its plans to make a splash in the AI data center business, through partnerships with companies like <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/991372/qualcomms-collaborating-with-amazon-on-multiple-generations-of-chips-for-aws-data-centers">Amazon</a> and <a href="https://www.qualcomm.com/news/releases/2026/06/qualcomm-and-meta-announce-strategic-multi-generation-agreement-">Meta</a> announced earlier this year. The company expects that portion of its business to grow quickly, forecasting around $650 million in data center revenue during the first quarter of 2027.
