<p class="wp-block-paragraph">OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic say they will adopt a new standard that publishers can use to opt out of being scraped by AI, as <a href="https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-training-control-42430.html">reported earlier by <em>Search Engine Roundtable</em></a>. With this control, publishers can apply their AI training preferences across multiple AI companies, instead of setting them individually.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The <a href="https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-kelley-aipref-training-preference-signal/">shared "policy token" signal</a> is proposed by Kevin Kelley, a technical lead for standards at Anthropic, as part of the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF). It will live within a website's <a href="https://www.theverge.com/24067997/robots-txt-ai-text-file-web-crawlers-spiders">robots.txt file</a>, which has long signaled which web crawlers can and can't scrape a site - whether for search indexing, archiving, or …</p>
