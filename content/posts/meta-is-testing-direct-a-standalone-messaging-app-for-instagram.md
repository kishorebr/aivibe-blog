                Meta is working on a dedicated messaging app for Instagram that could also expand the reach of the app's DMs. The company is starting to test Direct, a new standalone app that brings Instagram's inbox to a separate app, but doesn't require an existing Instagram account to use. Direct will only be available in Thailand and Turkey for now, Meta said in an update.
Direct looks pretty similar to Instagram's inbox at first glance. It has an almost identical layout, with existing chats carrying over from the main app if you log in with your Instagram credentials. It even has some of the same personalization features, like the ability to set custom backgrounds for individual threads and Instagram's status update feature, Notes.
