<p class="wp-block-paragraph">More AI agents will soon be able to browse and purchase from Shopify listings, marking an expansion of the company's agentic commerce ambitions.  </p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Shopify <a href="https://x.com/benjaminsehl/status/2110101061499539825">announced</a> Tuesday it will let shoppers <a href="https://shop.md/">configure their AI agents</a> to purchase products on their behalf, giving more agents access to over a billion listings in Shopify's catalog. (The catalog consists of both items on and off Shopify storefronts.) In September, Shopify <a href="https://www.wsj.com/tech/shopify-to-use-metas-muse-for-agentic-checkout-d23947c0">announced</a> it was partnering with Meta to allow its Muse agent to browse and purchase items on a human's behalf; now, shoppers can use any agent without a partnership integration.</p>
